What is Knobsock?

By Bobby Wyckoff · 8/29/2026
Photos by Wali Khan

I made Knobsock as a way to put all of my friends art on display under one umbrella so it’s easier for people to find and have sort of it’s own identity. Things are generally more fun when you do them with friends. When you work collectively with people you’re able to do far more than you would otherwise be able to do alone.

I Like Putting My Friend’s Art on Display

Social media has been confined to maybe 4 websites or apps that people flip between while eating Cheetos. Outside of the fact that it’s nearly impossible to share all of your work in one coherent space, we also noticed that when we choose to share our work on these websites we also completely lose control over how it’s hosted, how the audience engages with the work and also any possible censorship or whatever issues you can think a billion dollar company can come up with.

Knobsock is an Art Collective First and Foremost

It’s a website where my friends and I can all share our work in one giant portfolio to create a shared identity and common goal. We do not take brand deals. It’s entirely paid out of pocket and will remain that way. If it does eventually turn a profit it will operate as a worker co-op.

i’ve been working on knobsock.net and the whole thing for 7 years now.

We hope to get to a point where we can print a zine and host events ourselves.

Why The Name “Knobsock”? What The Hell Does That Mean?

From 2015 to 2020 I was living in a situation that wasn’t going too well for me. Without going into too much detail, the housing situation I was in led to multiple break-ins of my bedroom. These break-ins were sort of inevitable at the time and led to my door being busted and the doorknob removed.

In order to gain whatever privacy I could at the time, I would put a sock in the hole where the doorknob used to be. When I did that, all of a sudden the room became my space again. That space became a place where my friends and I could create all the art our heart desired. I had built that same space into a music studio and every Wednesday would record with friends. It only made sense that our space on the internet would also share the name.

I hope that I can use this space to showcase great art and lift people up in a way where we all share the wealth.

By wealth I don’t mean money, but community and the resources it takes to actually make said art.
