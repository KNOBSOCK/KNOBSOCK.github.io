Privacy Policy & User Agreement

Last updated September 8, 2026

This page explains what KNOBSOCK actually collects when you use this site, why, and how to get any of it deleted. I'm Bobby Wyckoff — I run this site, and I'd rather you actually read this than skim past a wall of legal boilerplate, so it's written the way I'd actually explain it to you.

What this site collects

The live chat. When you pick a username in the chat (Live / The Living Room), this site drops a small ID in your browser's local storage so it remembers you the next time you show up — that's what keeps your name yours without needing a password. Your username and whatever you send in chat get stored so the chat can actually work and so old messages can load back in.

The first time you ever claim a username, the site also does a one-time lookup of your public IP address. This isn't used to identify you — it exists purely so that if someone gets banned and immediately signs up under a new name to get back in, I have some way of noticing the pattern. It's stored as a scrambled (hashed) version of your address, not the address itself, and it's deleted automatically 90 days after you claim your name, whether you keep using the site or not.

Page views. The number on the homepage just counts how many times the site's been loaded. It isn't tied to you in any way.

The store. Checkout isn't live yet. Once it is, Stripe will handle your payment details directly — I will never see or store your card number myself.

That's everything I put here on purpose. This site does not run Google Analytics, ad trackers, or any tracking pixels of its own — it used to run Google Analytics and I turned it off. I don't have your name, email, or home address unless you send me one yourself. All of the above lives on Google Firebase, which is just where the data is hosted, not a separate party I'm sharing it with.

The video and music players on this site (Videos, Music Spot) are real embedded YouTube and SoundCloud players, not something I built myself. Playing something in them means you're interacting with YouTube's or SoundCloud's own player, and each of those sets its own cookies under its own privacy policy — that's between you and them, not something this site controls or sees.

Why there's no cookie banner

The only thing this site keeps in your browser is the device ID that remembers your chat username. That's not optional tracking you can opt out of and still use the site — it's what makes the chat work at all, the same way any site with accounts needs a way to recognize you're logged in. Since nothing here is optional, there's nothing that needs your consent before it loads.

Kids

This site isn't directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect anything from anyone that age. If you think a kid gave me information anyway, email me and I'll delete it.

Changes

If what this site collects ever changes, I'll update this page. The date at the top always reflects the current version.

Questions, or want to see or delete what I have on you — chat history, your IP hash, any of it? Email contact@knobsock.net and I'll take care of it.
