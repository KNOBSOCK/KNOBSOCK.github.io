Bulges the content pane outward from center, like a curved CRT. 0 is flat. Applies to the sample forum content below, not the glow.
How close to the true edge the glow starts. Lower = tighter/closer to the screen.
How far the glow spreads outward before fading out.

Extra blur applied after tinting, for smoothness.
Each clips a margin (as % of the image) off that side before the glow is allowed to show, so only the screen cutout glows instead of the whole image border. Raise a side if you see a glow leaking around the outer edge there; lower it if the glow looks cut off on that side. The art's cutout usually sits much closer to one edge (often the top) than the others, so these are rarely equal — that's expected, not a bug.
